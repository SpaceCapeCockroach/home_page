## About Me

I am a senior undergraduate student in Computer Science at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). Previously, I was an exchange student in the Parallel Undergraduate Studies program at UC San Diego (UCSD), where I conducted research at the **Existential Robotics Lab (ERL)** under the supervision of [Prof. Nikolay Atanasov](https://nikolay.atanasov.cc/).

My research interests lie broadly at the intersection of **Embodied AI**, **3D Vision-Language Mapping**, and **Robot Learning**. Specifically, I focus on:
* **3D Semantic Grounding & Spatial AI**
* **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Frameworks**
* **Deep Reinforcement Learning for Legged & Humanoid Locomotion**
