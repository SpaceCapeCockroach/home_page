## About Me

I am a senior undergraduate student in Computer Science at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). Currently, I am conducting research at the **SUSTech Visual Intelligence Laboratory**, under the guidance of [Prof. Feng Zheng](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/faculties/zhengfeng.html). Previously, I was an exchange student in the Parallel Undergraduate Studies program at UC San Diego (UCSD), where I worked at the **Existential Robotics Lab (ERL)** under the supervision of [Prof. Nikolay Atanasov](https://natanaso.github.io/).

My research interests lie broadly at the intersection of **Embodied AI**, **3D Vision-Language Mapping**, and **Robot Learning**. Specifically, I focus on:
* **3D Semantic Grounding & Spatial AI**
* **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Frameworks**
* **Deep Reinforcement Learning for Legged & Humanoid Locomotion**
